Which one to take

  • Exam 1 — mirrors the real format and spans all six scenarios evenly. Take it first.
  • Exams 2 and 3 — a random 4-of-6 scenario draw, closer to an actual sitting.
  • Exams 4, 5 and 6 — deliberately harder, with sharper distractors. A lower score here is expected.

How to use the score

Rough bands on raw score, not the real scaled 720 cut: 50+ you are close to ready · 40–49 review your weakest domains · below 40 go back to the tutor lessons and banks. The runner scores each domain separately, so read the table before the headline number — a 45 that hides a 30% domain is worse than a 45 that is even across all five.

Every miss shows the rationale inline, so review the questions you failed while the reasoning is still fresh.
