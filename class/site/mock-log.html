Raw-score bands (local mocks only)

These are rough gut-checks, not the real scaled 720/1000 cut.

  • 50+ — likely ready: final recall-card skim, then book.
  • 40-49 — solid, but repair the weakest domain before scheduling.
  • Below 40 — not ready: re-run the tutor lessons on the weak domains, then drill those banks.

Exams 4-6 are intentionally harder than the real thing, so a lower raw score there is expected and is not evidence of regression. Compare like with like.

Booking gate

Book only when all of these hold:

  1. Two independent mocks at 50+ raw (or 900+ on the external mocks).
  2. No single domain below 70% on any run.
  3. The miss log in PROGRESS.md shows no repeating trap type.

Taking a mock

Open the exam on the site, press begin, and do not pause the clock. Take the misses into PROGRESS.md's miss log with their trap type — the review screen names the task and domain for each.
